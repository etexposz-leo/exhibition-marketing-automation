Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers ET EXPO Marketing Automation, operated by ET EXPO INC, including its account-connection and content-review features. It does not replace the privacy policies of TikTok, Meta, LinkedIn, Google or other connected services.

2. Information processed

  • Login and account administration information, such as email address, account identifiers, password hashes and authentication records.
  • Information returned by an authorized social platform, such as account ID, display name, profile image, granted permissions and token expiration. For TikTok basic-profile authorization, this may include an Open ID, display name and avatar. We do not request your TikTok password.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed for authorized connections.
  • Content, media, campaign details and contact information you choose to submit, where those features are enabled.
  • Approval, job and error records used to operate and troubleshoot the service. Hosting services may process network and request metadata.

Only data made available by the enabled feature and the permissions you grant is retrieved. Adding a sandbox test user alone is not an authorization to collect their social account data.

3. Purposes and sharing

We use information to authenticate users, identify connected accounts, prepare and review content, carry out authorized operations, maintain security and diagnose failures. A provider receives the requests and content needed for a feature you use. Render provides hosting for this deployment. If an AI generation feature is configured and used, the selected AI provider receives the input required for that request.

We do not sell personal information or use connected-account data for advertising targeting. We may disclose information when necessary to meet applicable legal obligations or protect the service. Providers process data under their own policies, and processing may occur outside your location.

4. Storage and security

The application stores connected-account credentials using encryption and restricts access through authenticated account ownership. Browser connections use HTTPS. Security controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute protection. Do not send passwords, tokens or client secrets by email or chat.

5. Retention and deletion

Connection credentials and related records are retained while needed for the connection and service operation. Content and operational records may remain until removed or no longer needed. Limited records may be retained for security, backups or legal obligations; backups may not be erased immediately.

To request access, correction, account closure or deletion, email Leo@etexpous.com with the subject “Marketing data request” and identify the relevant account or platform. We will verify your authority and respond with the action taken or any retention limitations. Do not include secrets. You can also revoke the app in the connected platform's settings; revoking access does not automatically erase previously stored records.

6. Cookies and public pages

The application uses a session cookie for authentication. These public policy pages do not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts or forms. Third-party login pages use their own cookies and privacy practices.

7. Children and updates

This business service is intended for authorized adult users and is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child's information was submitted. Policy changes will appear here with an updated effective date.
